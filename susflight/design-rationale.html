Introduction

The Sustainable Flight Project aims to explore solutions for sustainable flight, especially regarding how we can make more informed and conscious travel decisions that minimally impact the environment. We decided to use sustainable flight because as Singaporeans, we automatically default to taking a flight out of the island when going anywhere, even places like Bali or KL.

Estimations

Estimated values:

  • Distance: Great-circle distance from Singapore Changi Airport
  • Aircraft Type: A320 if 3000km or lower, B789 if 3000-9000km, A359 if over 9000km
  • Fuel: OpenAP cruise fuel flow with 80% maximum takeoff weight with fixed amounts for takeoff and landing (800 kg for narrow-bodies, 2000 kg for wide-bodies)
  • CO2 Emissions: 3.16 kg CO2 per kg of fuel burned
  • Passenger Share: 90% for A320, 80% for B789 and A359
  • Passenger Load Factor: 80% for all aircraft types
